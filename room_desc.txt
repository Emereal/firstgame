Cubicle
This is your home... and your prison where you're destined to 
 work illegal wages until you somehow find a better job!! 
 There's a computer on the lock screen in front of you, along with
 papers scattered about and a coffee mug on the table you got from Jerry. 
 On second thought, you should probably report your boss for paying
 Jerry in pieces of gum.

Floor 1
Around you are a whole bunch of people looking at their colorful 
 cubicles in this bland office building, or taking breaks watching
 videos and chatting. 
 Usually you wouldn't talk much, but for some reason, it seems that
 you've magically forgotten where the bathroom is.
 Surely one of these empty-bladdered folk could help you out?

Floor 1?
Upon crawling up into the attic, the dust immediately hits you
 along with the feeling that there are definitely multiple
 spiders up here. Around you, you see multiple boxes and old
 appliances that we stored or didn't need until the winter.
 Spiderwebs connect between the boxes, as well as a single strip
 of light from the small window ahead. It's dusty up here, so
 don't launder around too long before you get sick.

Floor 1...
This, is The Office. No, not the show, but a room in your house
 used by your father. There are papers scattered across the desk
 in front of you, along with a photo of your parents years
 before you were born, both adorned in white. In the middle of
 the desk, a Dell computer sits idle with many messy cords
 hooked into it. When you try to open it, you realize that it's
 locked with a password that you do not know.

Floor 1.
The kitchen, your favorite place to go when you're hungry
specifically in the middle of the night. The island stands
between you and salvation, the fridge sitting right next to
the sink which seems to be dripping because someone didn't turn
it off correctly. You should go turn it off. There's also
plenty of items on the counter and many shelves you can search
for more items, maybe they'll help you get into the garage south
of here.

Cafeteria
This is a spacious area, with meals of varying levels of appeal
sitting in front of hungry workers.
The place seems a little like your highschool cafeteria, which
scares you slightly because you aren't sure if the food is safe
given the gum hidden under all the tables and the food stains
everywhere. Though even without those, you aren't sure if you'd
ever come to eat in this cafeteria.
After all, what point is there to a cafeteria without chocolate milk?
